# North Star — Evan's Meditation School

## What "done" looks like

1. A stranger finds the site via Google or ChatGPT, signs up to the mailing list, and receives the welcome sequence — without Evan touching anything.
2. A prospective student pays for a course and immediately sees their dashboard with all pre-course materials — no manual email, no password sharing.
3. After a live session, Evan pastes a Zoom recording URL into the admin and students can access it within minutes.
4. Evan can write and publish a blog post without a developer.
5. The mailing list grows passively because SEO content answers real meditation questions and captures email before a purchase decision is made.

## Worst-case scenario the system must survive

Evan teaches a live session and the site goes down while 30 students try to get the Zoom link from their dashboard. Recovery: static Zoom link visible on the course marketing page as fallback; Evan can email the list directly via MailChimp.

## What "working" means to a real student on a real day

They open their laptop 10 minutes before class, navigate to `/dashboard`, see their course with the Zoom link visible, click it, and join — without logging in twice, without a support email, without confusion about which link is current.
