## `/the-work` — IPF Facilitation & 1:1 Sessions

### Opening

**[LIFT]**
> "The patterns we develop in our earliest relationships can follow us for decades. They shape how we trust, connect, and see ourselves. When those patterns come from neglect, inconsistency, or trauma they can make it hard to feel safe in our own skin."

**[LIFT]**
> "The Ideal Parent Figure Protocol is a gentle, research-based, trauma-informed approach to repairing these early relational wounds. It works by creating new, internal experiences of care, protection and attunement, which give your nervous system the secure base it may have never had."

### How it works

**[LIFT]**
> "In IPF sessions we use guided imagery to install new internal working models of relating. You don't need to rehash painful memories or go into your life history in detail. Instead, we focus directly on the emotional and nervous system patterns that keep you stuck."

### Outcomes

- Move from anxious, avoidant, or disorganized attachment toward earned security
- Quiet a harsh inner critic and replace it with a kinder, more supportive inner voice
- Feel safer in your own body and more grounded in relationships
- Build resilience and emotional regulation without relying on suppression

### Who it's for

- Healing from early relational trauma or neglect
- Struggling with trust, vulnerability, or intimacy
- Caught in repeating patterns of self-criticism or emotional shutdown
- Living with the long-term effects of complex trauma (C-PTSD)
- Ready for a deep, lasting shift in their experience of life

### How we work together (3-step flow)

**[LIFT]**

1. **Free consultation** — 30 minutes to explore your needs, answer questions, and see if we're a fit
2. **Personalized plan** — A path forward tailored to your individual circumstances and capacities
3. **Regular 50-minute sessions** — The deep, experiential work of building secure attachment from within. Most clients meet every week or every other week.
